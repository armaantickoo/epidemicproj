Derivation for the reproductive number in the household $R_{0,\text{house}}$
$$
R_{0,\text{house}} =
\frac{E_h(1-\gamma)\beta_h}
{1-(1-\gamma)(1-\beta_h)}
$$
where $E_h$ is the expected number of susceptible people in the household, which can also be written as $/P_{avg}-1} where $P_{avg}$ is the average number of people in each household. 
$\gamma$ is the recovery probability, and
$\beta_h$ is the household infectivity rate.
